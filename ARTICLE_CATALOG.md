# Article Catalog — furnitur.co.id

This catalog plans a complete Indonesian furniture knowledge layer across classification, products, materials, construction, ergonomics, use cases, design, manufacture, finishing, procurement, installation, care, circularity, and safety. It is a planning inventory, not authorization to mass-publish thin pages.

Domain boundaries: furnitur.co.id owns furniture-specific understanding and decisions. Existing object, material, room, and sector routes retain transactional intent. Architectural interiors, building structure, general lighting engineering, and material fabrication unrelated to furniture are excluded. Same-domain intent collisions must be resolved; other owned domains may independently cover the same subject.

Catalog statistics: 15 parent topics, 120 planned articles, eight articles per topic. Wave A contains 12 bounded launch assets; Waves B–D are conditional expansions.

Priority legend: `P0` safety/high-value foundation or first-cluster dependency; `P1` core authority; `P2` specialist/later-lifecycle depth; `P3` useful long-tail or evidence-dependent expansion.
