# Topical Authority — furnitur.co.id

## Role and boundary

Furnitur.co.id should become an Indonesian decision and lifecycle reference for movable and built-in furniture: understanding types, specifying products, selecting materials and construction, planning rooms, procuring responsibly, accepting completed work, and keeping furniture safe and serviceable. The primary audience is Indonesian homeowners, tenants, designers, facility and procurement teams, hospitality/health/education operators, and buyers considering ready-made or custom work.

The knowledge layer is neutral and evidence-led. Existing product, room, material, and project-type routes retain commercial intent; editorial articles may explain the same entity but must not become disguised sales pages. Building structure, architectural interiors as a whole, lighting design as a discipline, and material fabrication unrelated to furniture are outside the core boundary. Cannibalization is evaluated only within furnitur.co.id. Separately owned domains may independently cover the same query.

Geographic assumptions are Indonesian: humid tropical interiors, termites and fungi, coastal corrosion, constrained urban access, local material terminology, custom-workshop procurement, and current Indonesian safety or conformity requirements where applicable. Exact legal and standard claims require primary-source verification at publication.

## Existing coverage and risks

Most existing material, object, room, and facility pages are commercial templates. They contain useful starting vocabulary but do not prove neutral topical coverage. Recurring “jual”, model, material, benefit, and quote language creates high outline similarity within each template family. The two posts are broad surveys whose material, style, sustainability, room, and trend passages overlap planned specialist pages.

Specific risks:

- `/berita` and `/category/blog/` appear to be overlapping archives.
- The two post files end in `.html`, while their canonical tags and `sitemap-complete.xml` use extensionless URLs; `post-sitemap.xml` uses `.html`.
- `/kaca/` redirects client-side to `/bahan/kaca`; `/author/syamsul-alam/` redirects client-side to `/`.
- `video-sitemap.xml` includes `/kamar`, while the repository route is `/kamar-tidur/`.
- The 24 material pages can collide with neutral material guides unless commercial and informational intent are explicit.
- Lamps appear in a furniture catalog even though lighting-system design is a separate discipline.
- Medical, religious, and public-use pages need stronger evidence than generic templates because cleaning, accessibility, stability, fire, and crowd-use requirements can matter.
- Claims about prices, dimensions, material emissions, flame performance, accessibility, or standards must not be generalized without dated primary evidence.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Use current primary sources for BSN/SNI status, Indonesian regulations, manufacturer limits, recalls, accessibility, product conformity, chemical safety, emissions, flame performance, and material legality. Name no clause from memory.
- Disclose whether dimensions are standards, manufacturer values, measured examples, population ranges, or design heuristics. Avoid universal “ideal” numbers.
- Material comparisons must identify grade/species/construction, finish, environment, load, and maintenance assumptions.
- Price and lead-time content must be dated, sourced, and expressed as components or scenario inputs unless a real current quotation is available.
- Safety procedures require stop conditions. Do not encourage unverified structural repair, glass work, spring/mechanism disassembly, energized furniture work, spray finishing without controls, or anchoring into unknown substrates.
- Health, infection-control, fire, and environmental claims need stronger evidence than marketing copy.
- Case studies require real project records, constraints, photos with rights, decisions, and outcomes. Label illustrative calculations as examples, never field results.
- AI-assisted drafts require human fact review, original diagrams/photos where promised, source logging, and same-domain overlap review before publication.
- Review time-sensitive articles at least annually and immediately after a relevant regulator, standard, manufacturer, or recall change.
